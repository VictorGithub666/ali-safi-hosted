# Rider Orders Feature - Quick Start Guide

## What's New? 🎉

The Rider Dashboard now displays available orders with:
- **Distance Information** - See exactly how far you need to travel
- **Delivery Estimates** - Know how long each delivery will take
- **Detailed Destinations** - Full address, customer contact, and special instructions
- **Better Layout** - Mobile-friendly card design with all info at a glance

## How to Use

### 1. Go Online
Click the **Availability** button in the top-right to toggle your status to "Available"
- The button will turn **GREEN** when you're online
- Location tracking starts automatically

### 2. View Available Orders
Scroll down to see all available orders. Each order card shows:

#### 📏 Distance Information
- **Pickup Distance** - How far from your current location to the vendor
- **Delivery Distance** - How far from vendor to customer
- **Total Distance** - The complete trip distance
- **Estimated Time** - How long the delivery should take

#### 📍 Destination Details
- Full delivery address with area
- Customer's phone number
- Recipient name
- Any special delivery instructions

#### 💰 Earnings
- Delivery fee clearly displayed
- Compare fee with distance to assess profitability

### 3. Accept an Order
Click the green **"Accept Order"** button when you find a profitable delivery

The order will be assigned to you and you'll start earning!

### 4. View More Details
Click **"View Details"** if you need to see additional order information

## Distance Estimates

**How distances are calculated:**
- Uses your GPS location (updated every 10 seconds while online)
- Measures direct distance from you → vendor → customer
- Estimates delivery time at average 30 km/h urban speed

**Examples:**
- `450m` = Less than 1 km away
- `2.5 km` = About 5 minutes drive
- `8.3 km` = About 15-20 minutes drive

## Tips for Maximum Earnings

✅ **Go online regularly** - More available orders appear when you're online
✅ **Compare distances** - Higher fees for longer distances usually mean better earnings
✅ **Complete orders quickly** - More completed orders = more ratings and trust
✅ **Keep location accurate** - Ensure GPS is enabled for accurate distance calculations
✅ **Update location manually** - Use "Update Location Now" button if needed

## Troubleshooting

### Not seeing distances?
- Make sure you've enabled location permissions in your browser
- Go back online (toggle availability)
- Refresh the page (F5)

### Distances seem wrong?
- Make sure your device's GPS is working
- Click "Update Location Now" button
- Clear browser cache and refresh

### Orders not showing up?
- Check that you're online (Green availability button)
- Make sure vendors in your area have active orders
- Refresh the page or wait a few moments for new orders

## Location Privacy

Your location is:
- ✅ Used only to calculate distances to available orders
- ✅ Updated in real-time while you're online
- ✅ Stored securely for your delivery tracking
- ✅ Never shared with customers until order accepted
